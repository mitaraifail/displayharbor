How it works

Three steps,
back in place.

  1. 01

    Open your apps

    Arrange your windows the way you like. DisplayHarbor reads the layout without taking over your content.

  2. 02

    Save the environment

    Give this display and window combination a name you will recognize, such as “Office · Work” or “Study · Learning”.

  3. 03

    Return in one click

    Switch back to the environment, open its scene, and restore your windows. App quit rules can come along too.

Get started
